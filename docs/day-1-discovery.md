# Day 1 discovery and baseline

Target user: a small-team recruiter or coordinator preparing a packet for human review. The workflow modeled from the specification is Form receipt, CV retrieval, field normalization, criterion evidence lookup, discrepancy check, and administrative next action. No recruiter interview or direct observation has occurred. Pain and time savings are hypotheses.

Baseline protocol: for each valid synthetic case, a recruiter or proxy receives the original Form answers and CV, starts a timer, writes normalized fields and criterion statuses with exact citations, notes ambiguities, and stops when the packet is reviewable. Record timestamps, corrections, and packet. All baseline values are pending until observed.
